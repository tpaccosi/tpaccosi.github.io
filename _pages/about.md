My name is Teresa Paccosi. I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [KNAW Humanities Cluster](https://huc.knaw.nl/) in Amsterdam, where I work within the Trofecta project.

My research sits between **computational linguistics** and the **digital humanities**. I am especially interested in how people talk about the senses: how smell and taste are described in texts, how to model those descriptions with FrameNet-inspired annotation schemes, and how they have changed over time. More recently I have been working on the language of **food and culinary history**, using historical recipes and texts to track how food and its uses evolve.

Before Amsterdam, I did my PhD in Cognitive Science at the University of Trento, as part of the Digital Humanities group at [Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK)](https://dh.fbk.eu/). There I worked on extracting olfactory information from multilingual historical texts, alongside work on Italian NLP resources such as named entity recognition datasets and treebanks. I hold an MA in Linguistics from the University of Siena, with a thesis in theoretical syntax.

Research interests
------
* Sensory language: smell and taste in text
* Semantic annotation and frame semantics
* Information extraction from historical texts
* Diachronic language change
* Language resources and NLP for Italian
